# E-Commerce Customer Behavior Analysis

End-to-end data science project analyzing 540k transactions from a 
UK-based wholesale gift retailer (UCI Online Retail Dataset).

## Key Findings
- Business is **B2B wholesale**, not consumer retail — confirmed through
  order size, timing patterns, and zero Saturday activity
- Top **26% of customers generate 80% of revenue** (Pareto principle)
- **34% of customers never return** after first purchase
- Average **Month 1 retention: 20.6%** — those who return become stable
  long-term buyers with retention holding at 24% through month 6
- November 2011 revenue (£1.16M) nearly **double** the summer average —
  strong Christmas seasonality

## Project Structure
- **Phase 1** — Data cleaning & exploratory analysis
- **Phase 2** — Customer behavior & revenue distribution
- **Phase 3** — RFM segmentation (Champions, Loyal, At Risk, Lost)
- **Phase 4** — Cohort retention analysis & heatmap
- **Phase 5** — Business recommendations

## Tools
Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · Seaborn · Google Colab

## Dataset
[UCI Online Retail Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail)
— 541,909 transactions, Dec 2010 to Dec 2011
